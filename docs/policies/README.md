## How to Use This Directory

1. **Finding a Policy**: Use the policy index above or browse by category
2. **Policy Format**: All policies follow the standard template in `/docs/templates/POLICY_TEMPLATE.md`
3. **Policy Lifecycle**:
   - **Draft**: Under development
   - **Review**: Under review by stakeholders
   - **Active**: Approved and in effect
   - **Archived**: No longer in effect
4. **Updates**: Policies are reviewed annually or as needed
5. **Compliance**: All employees must acknowledge and comply with applicable policies

## Policy Development Process

1. Identify need for new policy or policy update
2. Create draft using policy template
3. Review with stakeholders
4. Obtain approval from policy owners and leadership
5. Communicate policy to affected parties
6. Implement and enforce
7. Monitor and review periodically
