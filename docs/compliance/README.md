## Audit and Assessment

### Internal Audits
- **Frequency**: Quarterly
- **Scope**: All critical controls
- **Process**: 
  1. Planning and scoping
  2. Control testing
  3. Issue identification
  4. Remediation tracking
  5. Management reporting

### External Audits
- **Frequency**: Annually
- **Scope**: Per audit standard
- **Process**:
  1. Auditor selection
  2. Planning meeting
  3. Evidence collection
  4. Control testing
  5. Findings review
  6. Report issuance

### Audit Evidence

#### Types of Evidence
- **Documentation**: Policies, procedures, standards
- **Artifacts**: Logs, screenshots, reports
- **Interviews**: Staff interviews and attestations
- **Observations**: Walkthroughs and demonstrations
- **Testing**: Control testing results

#### Evidence Requirements
- Relevant to control objective
- Reliable and trustworthy
- Sufficient to support conclusion
- Properly organized and indexed
- Retained per requirements

## Issue Management

### Issue Tracking
1. **Identification**: Finding identified
2. **Assessment**: Impact and priority
3. **Assignment**: Owner assigned
4. **Remediation**: Corrective action
5. **Validation**: Remediation verified
6. **Closure**: Finding closed

### Priority Levels
- **Critical**: Major compliance gap, immediate action
- **High**: Significant gap, 30-day remediation
- **Medium**: Moderate gap, 90-day remediation
- **Low**: Minor gap, next audit cycle
